# Name and logo

The source code is licensed under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **buymeatokens** name, the logo and the
`buymeatokens.com` domain are not, because they identify the hosted service run by the maintainers.

**You're welcome to:**

- say that your project or fork is "based on buymeatokens" or "a fork of buymeatokens";
- use the name and logo to link to buymeatokens.com or to this repository, including in articles and talks;
- keep the name in code, comments and the git history of your fork.

**Please don't:**

- run a public service under the buymeatokens name or logo, or a confusingly similar one;
- suggest that your fork, service or product is official or endorsed by us when it isn't.

If you self-host for yourself or your team, you don't have to rename anything. If you're unsure, just ask at
hello@buymeatokens.com.
